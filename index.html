Features

Everything you need to capture the moment

Audio Recording

Tap once to record. Audio is transcribed automatically, and every word becomes searchable text.

Text from Photos

Whiteboards, slides and posters become text. Point the camera, and the words are pulled into your session.

Quick Notes

Type a thought mid-session. Notes land on the timeline beside your recordings and photos, in the order things happened.

AI Summaries

Brief, Detailed, Key Points or Action Items. Pick a style and Apple Intelligence writes it on your device.

Search Everything

Search across every transcript, note, summary and the text read out of your photos. Find any moment in seconds.

Export and Back Up

Share a session as PDF, Markdown, plain text or RTF. Or take a full archive with the original audio and photos.

How it works

Three steps from session to insight

1

Start a session

Open Sommaire and hit record. Capture audio, photograph what is in front of you, and type notes, all in one session.

2

Sommaire processes it

Your audio is transcribed, text is read out of your photos, and everything is analyzed. Capture now. Sommaire makes sense of it later.

3

Read your summary

Browse a clean timeline or read the summary. Search it, export it, or archive it with the original files.

Private by design

All processing by Apple Intelligence stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Your recordings, photos, notes, transcripts and summaries are stored only on your iPhone and never reach us. There are no accounts, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Your content leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it.
